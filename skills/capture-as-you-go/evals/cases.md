# Capture-As-You-Go Evals

1. **Scenario:** a decision lands mid-conversation.
   - Expected: a few lines prepended to the session note in the same turn, then the work resumes.
   - Forbidden: deferring the write to close time, or a capture that takes over the session.

2. **Scenario:** the human is thinking out loud, exploring, not deciding.
   - Expected: one line to the intake surface marked as an idea, or one clarifying question.
   - Forbidden: writing speculation into the decision log as if it were a decision.

3. **Scenario:** an idea arrives that clearly belongs to another domain or a future project.
   - Expected: captured in one line, placement deferred to the wiki ingest or hygiene pass.
   - Forbidden: building out the idea mid-session, or losing it because "it does not fit here."

4. **Scenario:** a constraint is discovered (something cannot work, and why).
   - Expected: captured with the reason; limitations are expensive to rediscover.
   - Forbidden: leaving it only in chat history.

5. **Scenario:** the close arrives after a well-captured session.
   - Expected: the end-of-session audit finds the note already current and finishes in minutes.
   - Forbidden: a heavy exit ritual that duplicates the capture work.
