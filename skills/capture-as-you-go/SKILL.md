# Capture As You Go

**Why:** the reasoning is freshest at the moment of decision. Capture then costs seconds. Capture at close means reconstruction from a fading context, and fidelity dies there. Continuous capture is also the honest record: every decision is the decision made. A later decision supersedes it. It does not erase it.

## What gets captured, the moment it happens

- **Decisions**: the call made, the rejected alternative when it matters, the why in one line. Link the tracker item when one exists.
- **Ideas worth keeping**: one line, captured now, placed later. Never let a live idea die because it arrived mid-flow.
- **Limitations and constraints**: what cannot work, and why. These age well and are expensive to rediscover.
- **Verified knowledge**: a fact that survived a check, with its source.
- **Progress markers** when a milestone completes: what exists now that did not exist before.

## How

- Decisions prepend a few lines to the session-notes file. No ceremony.
- Ideas and half-formed thoughts go to the intake surface: the workspace's parking file or a tracker item. Never into the decision log.
- Placement is provisional. The wiki ingest and hygiene passes move things to where they belong.
- If capture takes more than a minute, it is a project, not a capture. Split it out and continue.

## What this is not

- Not a status writer. The tracker owns status.
- Not a refactoring pass. Do not reformat earlier entries mid-session.
- Not a mind reader. Thinking out loud is not a decision. Mark it as an idea, or ask one question.
- Not an interruption. Capture in one turn, then return to the work.

## Completion criterion

The decision is on disk in the same turn it was made, and the work resumed.
